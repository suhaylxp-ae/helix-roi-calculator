Change & Problem capacity cash if cost filled

Effort cut first. Failed-change reduction is cash if you enter a cost per failure; otherwise it is upside. Problem hours are separate.

Network Management capacity

Onboarding hours and false-alert suppression. If AIOps is also selected, exclude these alerts from the AIOps count.

Discovery / CMDB / Asset Management capacity cash if filled

Record-update and audit hours. Reclaimed unused assets are cash only if you enter a count and unit cost. Breach risk is qualitative upside — not valued.

Breach-risk reduction is upside text only and is not converted to money in this model.

Server Automation (TSSA) capacity

Patch, vulnerability, and compliance labour. If Change is also selected, exclude patch changes from the Change volume.

Continuous Optimization (BHCO) true cash

This module is one of the few that produces true cash (cloud, hardware, storage) rather than only released time. Capacity-planning hours stay in the capacity ledger. Capacity outages are upside only.

ESM / Business Workflows capacity

HR, finance, and facilities cases — not IT tickets. Deflection first, then rework and status-enquiry cuts on remaining cases.
