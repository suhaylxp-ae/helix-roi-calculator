      How we count value

      Cash, counted staff time, and extra benefit (not counted) are kept apart. Headline ROI is cash plus counted staff time. Adoption grows over time: 50% in year 1, 85% in year 2, 100% after. We count only 50–70% of saved time. Hours cannot exceed the baseline in that area. Each saving is counted once. Outage costs are not counted in ROI unless you turn on Include outage savings. That line is tagged Outage savings (your own figure). It uses your cost per minute, grows in over time (50 / 85 / 100%), is not cut by the 50–70% time rule, and is capped at half of yearly outage cost.

      Simple-mode ratios (illustrative; edit the derived fields here to override):

      • Tickets / year = tickets per month × 12. Alerts / year = alerts per day × 365. Major outages / year = major outages per month × 12.
      • Changes / year = 5% of IT tickets. Problems / year = 0.375% of IT tickets.
      • Auto-remediable events = 5.7% of annual alerts. Network alerts = 43% of annual alerts when Network is on (then subtracted from AIOps).
      • Emergency team calls / year = major outages × 20 / 72. Knowledge articles and reporting hours = IT staff × 5. ESM cases = IT staff × 150.
      • Network devices = IT staff × 31. New devices = IT staff × 5. Assets = IT staff × 312. Asset updates = IT staff × 250.
      • Service models = IT staff × 0.5. BHCO planning hours = IT staff × 7.5. Monthly vulns = servers × 40 / 1,800.
      • Annual hardware refresh = 18.5% of annualised cloud. Annual storage = 4.2% of annualised cloud.
